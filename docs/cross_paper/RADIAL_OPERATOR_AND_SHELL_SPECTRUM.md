# Corrected radial operator and conditional shell spectrum

PFF P02 §§2.3,6 introduces O_Θ=−r⁻¹∂r(r∂r)+m_ang²/r²+V_Θ(r) kinematically. The revised Hessian P10 §§3–7 supplies a rigorous fourth-order variational method and warns that a local negative second-order coefficient is not by itself an instability theorem. Here that method is composed with **S004's actual weight**, rather than transferring its older scalar denominator.

## Exact new static Hessian for a real radial background

For static ψ₀=a(x) real, write u=a², b=Δa and C=κ+θ+γ>0. The positive-ordinary-kinetic static energy is

\[
E=\int[|\nabla\psi|^2-V(|\psi|^2)+C F(|\psi|^2)|\Delta\psi|^2].\tag{R1}
\]

Expand ψ=a+r+iv about an on-shell background. The coefficient of the squared perturbation is

\[
Q_r=\int[a_4(x)(\Delta r)^2+|\nabla r|^2+B_1r\Delta r+B_2r^2],
\]
\[
a_4=CF,\quad B_1=4CaF'b,\quad
B_2=-V'-2uV''+C(F'+2uF'')b^2.
\]

Twice integrating the mixed term, with the boundary form retained as in Q2, gives

\[
\boxed{L_r=\Delta(a_4\Delta)-\nabla\cdot(R\nabla)+W,
\quad R=1-B_1,\quad W=B_2+\tfrac12\Delta B_1.}\tag{R2}
\]

The phase block is

\[
L_v=\Delta(a_4\Delta)-\Delta-V'+CF'b^2.\tag{R3}
\]

For a prescribed fixed-L² constraint subtract its multiplier from both W terms and project the tangent space, as P10 requires; no such constraint is silently added to S004. The static real-background blocks decouple, whereas rotating/winding backgrounds generally mix. R2 is exact under the stated background and boundary assumptions, not a local frozen-coefficient approximation. It is **new to this audit**. [a₄]=L²,[R]=1,[W]=L⁻².

For a radial coefficient set in two dimensions and angular mode e^{im_ang φ}, let D_m=∂r²+r⁻¹∂r−m_ang²/r². Then

\[
L_{r,m}f=D_m(a_4D_m f)-r^{-1}\partial_r(rR\partial_rf)
+R\,m_{\rm ang}^2r^{-2}f+Wf.\tag{R4}
\]

The measure is r dr, with regularity at zero and declared decay/boundary conditions. This supplies the actual radial operator generated by the corrected action. Its coefficients require a self-consistent a(r); they are not independently chosen cavity walls. It is fourth order. If a→0, then F→0, so uniform fourth-order ellipticity cannot be borrowed from P10's assumption h≥h₀>0.

## Can this become PFF's second-order operator?

For constant a₄, an exact square L_r=a₄(−Δ+V_Θ)²+Δ_gap requires

\[
R=2a_4V_\Theta,\qquad
W=a_4(V_\Theta^2-\Delta V_\Theta)+\Delta_{\rm gap}.\tag{R5}
\]

These follow by expanding the operator on a test function, including derivatives of V_Θ. A generic S004 profile does not satisfy these identities. Variable a₄ introduces additional derivative structure. Thus V_Θ=R/(2a₄) alone is not a reduction: its W and derivative constraints must also hold. The homogeneous EFT has the special constant choice V_Θ=−q_star after branch normalization. A local frozen symbol a₄q²+Rq+W gives q_local=−R/(2a₄) if R<0, but is only a diagnostic with error controlled by background variation scale 1/(kL_bg); it is not a global spectrum or a derived potential well.

If an independently established self-adjoint radial H has discrete eigenvalues Λ_n, then the spectral theorem gives (H−q_star)²f_n=(Λ_n−q_star)²f_n exactly. This supports a conditional cross-paper composition, but supplies neither H's confinement nor its domain. A free whole-space radial Laplacian has continuous spectrum. A prescribed Dirichlet disk has Λ_{mn}=j_{mn}²/R² and imports R. A derived decaying well may have discrete levels below a continuum; its entire spectrum need not be discrete.

## Shell quantization: what the wavenumber means

For the declared second-order PFF operator, put f=w/√r. Then

\[
-w''+\left[V_\Theta+\frac{m_{\rm ang}^2-1/4}{r^2}\right]w=\Lambda w.\tag{R6}
\]

In a two-simple-turning-point WKB regime, the radial oscillation action obeys

\[
\int_{r_-}^{r_+}\sqrt{\Lambda-V_\Theta-(m_{\rm ang}^2-1/4)/r^2}\,dr
\simeq\pi(n_r+1/2),\tag{R7}
\]

with turning-point matching and small adiabatic parameter |k_r′|/k_r² away from the turning points. Endpoint singularities and hard walls change the phase corrections. This is an oscillation-action statement, not an oriented out-and-back integral of a single-valued phase gradient.

PFF's printed Eq. (13), ∮k_r dr=2πΛ_n, has a dimensionless left side and an L⁻² right side unless Λ is explicitly nondimensionalized. Even after nondimensionalization a Schrödinger eigenvalue is not generally an action integer. R6–R7 give a conditional corrected spectral relation. Spatial phase winding ∮∇ϑ·dl=2πn on a nonzero-field loop is separately exact.

The amplitude carrier k_min from F2 does not by itself supply the Goldstone phase gradient or the k_r in R7. A self-consistent bound background, factorization/reduction conditions, and its boundary data must connect them. No radial eigenvalue or winding sector has been selected here.
