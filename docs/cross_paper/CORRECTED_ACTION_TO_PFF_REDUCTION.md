For the controlled witness Δ₀>0. Therefore σ_max=−ΓΔ₀<0. The map yields a **least-damped finite-k band**, not the growing PFF band. Actual onset requires Δ₀<0 or an independent pumping/detuning term. Likewise β requires a nonlinear normal-form calculation including V‴,V⁗ and field normalization; no numeric β follows from a quadratic Hessian.

For a one-dimensional complex single carrier A=ε¹ᐟ²B(X,T)e^{ik_star x}, μ=εμ₁, X=ε¹ᐟ²x,T=εt, the declared PFF rail gives at leading order

\[
\partial_TB=\mu_1B+4bk_\star^2\partial_X^2B-\beta|B|^2B.

On a two-dimensional isotropic critical circle, transverse modulation has a different scaling and a single isotropic Δ_X amplitude equation is not automatic.
