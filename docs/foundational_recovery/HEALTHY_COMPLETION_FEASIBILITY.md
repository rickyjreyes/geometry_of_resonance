    det[(omega^2-|k|^2)G0-W''0]=0.

ordinary component wave energies in the chosen spacelike slicing. Commuting

## A2. Purely spatial boundary: defined, but not a recovered foundation

The original corrected draft itself lists purely spatial curvature dynamics
as a distinct possibility. On the nonnegative-form boundary kappa=gamma=0,
theta>0, the specified fixed-background action becomes

    L_sp=|psi_tau|^2-|grad psi|^2-U(rho)-theta f(rho)|Delta psi|^2,
    psi_tautau-Delta psi+U' psi
             +theta[f' psi|Delta psi|^2+Delta(f Delta psi)]=0.

It has velocity Hessian 2I, acceleration Hessian zero, momenta pi=2 dot x,
no constraints and two real configuration degrees. H_sp=integral
[|pi|^2/4+|grad x|^2+U+theta f|Delta x|^2] and Q=integral pi.Jx.
For U>=0 it has nonnegative energy. About a nonzero constant stationary
background, the two symbols are omega^2=q+theta f0 q^2+m^2, with m^2=0 in
phase and m^2=2rho U'' in amplitude. High-frequency evolution there is stable;
nonnegative m^2 is an additional infrared condition. No finite-q minimum is
created by these positive coefficients.

This removes the offending term by changing the allowed action parameters;
it is outside S004's strict positivity hypotheses. It also lacks a global
H^2 estimate through nodes. On a 2pi-periodic coordinate take epsilon=1,
alpha=0, U=0, psi_n=sin(nx)/n. Then |grad psi_n|<=1 and

    f(psi_n^2)|Delta psi_n|^2
      =sin^4(nx)/(1+sin^2(nx)/n^2)^2 <=1,

whereas ||partial_xx psi_n||_2^2=pi n^2. Bounded energy does not control H^2.
This is a static noncoercivity sequence, not time blowup. General nonlinear
well-posedness with variable/vanishing f needs additional analysis. The positive
spatial dilation obstruction also survives for U=0. Hence this boundary is
not accepted as a complete healthy confinement theory.
