# Scope and Non-Claims of Wave Confinement Theory (WCT)

**Author:** Richard J. Reyes  
**Document type:** scope audit / claim-boundary statement  
**Purpose:** define what WCT claims, what it does not claim, and which parts are established, conjectural, or deliberately open.

---

## 1. Minimal Scope

Wave Confinement Theory (WCT) is a wave-first theoretical framework. Its fundamental object is a confined complex wavefield

\[
\psi(x,t)
\]

evolving under nonlinear curvature feedback. WCT studies how mass-like inertia, coherent spectral structure, and dimensional stability can arise from self-confined wave dynamics rather than being inserted as primitive assumptions.

The central WCT claim is not that all known physics has already been replaced. The central claim is narrower:

> Self-confined, phase-coherent standing-wave solutions governed by nonlinear curvature feedback can generate effective mass, stable spectral shells, and dimensional stability constraints.

---

## 2. Core Operator

The representative WCT curvature-feedback operator is

\[
\Theta[\psi]
= -\frac{\nabla^2\psi}{\psi + \varepsilon e^{-\alpha |\psi|^2}},
\qquad
\varepsilon>0,
\quad
\alpha>0.
\]

Here:

- \(\psi\) is the wavefield.
- \(\nabla^2\psi\) measures local spatial curvature of the field.
- \(\varepsilon e^{-\alpha |\psi|^2}\) regularizes the denominator near nodes.
- \(\Theta[\psi]\) acts as a dynamical feedback term, not merely as a diagnostic quantity.

WCT uses this operator, or controlled variants of it, to model curvature-regulated localization, spectral organization, and confinement stability.

## 3. What WCT Explicitly Claims

### 3.1 Emergent Mass from Wave Confinement

Mass is not assumed as a primitive input. It appears when propagating wave energy becomes self-confined into a structurally stable, phase-coherent standing-wave configuration.

Symbolically, a closed-loop confinement channel with curvature \(\kappa\), torsion \(\tau\), and density weight \(w(s)\) yields an effective curvature scale

\[
\sigma(s)=\sqrt{\kappa(s)^2+\tau(s)^2},
\]

and a mass law of the form

\[
m = \frac{\hbar}{c}\langle \sigma \rangle_w.
\]

Thus mass is identified with a curvature-regulated resonance scale of the confined wavefield.

### 3.2 Self-Generated Curvature Feedback

Curvature is not imposed as a fixed background. The wavefield generates effective curvature through its own spatial derivatives and amplitude structure. That curvature feeds back on \(\psi\), regulating localization, mode locking, and stability.

### 3.3 Nonlinear Mode Selection

WCT claims that confined wavefields can organize into discrete shells, harmonics, and stable coherent modes. These structures arise only after confinement, feedback, and boundary/topological constraints are present.

This is not a generic claim that every nonlinear wave minimizes entropy or returns to a unique prior state. The claim is about persistent coherent structure: bounded confinement, phase-locking basins, and spectral mode organization.

### 3.4 Spatial Dimensionality Bound

WCT claims that long-lived curvature-locked confined solutions impose a stability bound on spatial dimensionality. In the WCT framework, three spatial dimensions arise as the maximal dimension admitting stable long-lived confined resonances.

The dimensional claim is a stability claim, not a claim that higher-dimensional mathematical spaces cannot be written down.

### 3.5 Curvature-Harmonic Mass Relations

Once confinement and shell structure are established, curvature harmonics can encode particle-like mass relations, including Koide-type triplet relations. These are downstream consequences of the confinement model, not independent postulates.

## 4. What WCT Does Not Claim

### 4.1 Not a Complete Replacement for the Standard Model

WCT does not presently reproduce the full Standard Model, including all interaction vertices, full gauge dynamics, renormalization machinery, measured scattering amplitudes, and precision electroweak phenomenology.

WCT addresses possible geometric origins of mass, curvature, dimensionality, and spectral organization. It does not yet constitute a complete Standard Model replacement.

### 4.2 Not a Complete Quantum Field Theory

WCT does not currently claim to be a fully quantized field theory. Quantization is treated as an emergent or effective description to be developed, not as a completed foundational axiom.

### 4.3 Not a Theory of Cosmological Initial Conditions

WCT does not derive the initial state of the universe, inflationary dynamics, dark-sector composition, or specific cosmological boundary conditions.

### 4.4 Not Experimental Confirmation

WCT proposes testable mechanisms and signatures, but it does not claim final experimental verification. Existing simulations, optical experiments, and open-data tests are evidence-generating probes, not conclusive confirmation.

### 4.5 Not a Proof of Uniqueness of Physical Law

WCT does not claim that wave confinement is the only possible foundation for physics. It claims that wave confinement is a structurally minimal candidate framework capable of generating mass, curvature, coherent modes, and dimensional constraints.

### 4.6 Not Guaranteed State Recovery

WCT does not assert that perturbed states must return to a unique pre-perturbation waveform. Stability means persistence of coherent structure and bounded confinement, not exact microscopic state recovery.

## 6. Established Within the WCT Framework

The following are established as internal WCT results, conditional on the framework's definitions and assumptions:

- Curvature-regularized operators can be defined on wavefields with node regularization.
- Effective mass scales can be written as curvature-locked resonance scales.
- Closed-loop phase locking yields a natural relation between winding, curvature, and rest energy.
- Spatial dimensionality enters stability through Sobolev/scaling constraints.
- Confined systems can select discrete spectral shells under finite-band dynamics.
- Curvature-harmonic mass relations follow once shell structure and confinement geometry are assumed.

---

## 7. Conjectural or Incomplete Elements

The following remain incomplete, conjectural, or dependent on future derivation and validation:

- Full dynamical derivation of all known interaction forces.
- Complete embedding into quantum measurement theory.
- Derivation of all observed particle families and couplings.
- Precision prediction of \(\alpha\), \(e\), \(m_e\), and related constants without remaining free geometric inputs.
- Fully controlled laboratory realization of curvature-locked confinement states.
- Independent replication of long-lived water-cavity resonance signatures.
- Official-covariance statistical tests for collider/log-periodic phenomenology.
- Formal cryptographic security proof for curvature-locked one-way functions.

---

## 8. Deliberately Open Problems

The following are left open by design:

- The microscopic origin of the universal wave medium.
- The exact relationship between WCT curvature and classical spacetime metrics in all regimes.
- The correspondence between WCT operators and canonical quantum observables.
- The full classification of confined solutions across topology and boundary conditions.
- The transition from WCT classical field dynamics to a complete quantum theory.
- The precise experimental protocol required to distinguish WCT confinement from conventional cavity, thermal, optical, or detector artifacts.

These omissions are scope boundaries, not hidden assumptions.

# Minimal Core of WCT

The following five postulates form the audit-compressed kernel of WCT. Removing any one collapses the theory.

## Postulate 1 — Wave-First Ontology

There exists a fundamental complex wavefield \(\psi\) defined on a continuous domain. Particles, masses, and spacetime geometry are not assumed as primitive entities.

## Postulate 2 — Confinement-Induced Mass

When wave propagation becomes self-confined into a structurally stable, phase-coherent standing-wave configuration, an effective inertial mass emerges. Mass is identified with the resonance scale of confinement.

## Postulate 3 — Self-Generated Curvature Feedback

The wavefield generates effective curvature through nonlinear dependence on its spatial derivatives and amplitude. This curvature feeds back dynamically on \(\psi\), regulating localization, stability, and coherent spectral organization.

## Postulate 4 — Curvature-Regulated Stability

Stable confined solutions exist only when curvature feedback balances dispersion. This balance selects discrete spectral shells and prevents both singular collapse and unbounded expansion.

This stability refers to persistent coherent structure and bounded confinement, not necessarily minimization of spectral entropy.

## Postulate 5 — Dimensional Stability Constraint

The existence of stable curvature-locked confined solutions imposes an upper bound on spatial dimensionality. Three spatial dimensions arise as the maximal dimension admitting long-lived confined resonances.

## Consequences, Not Postulates

The following are downstream consequences or optional extensions, not primitive assumptions:

- Emergent spacetime geometry.
- Curvature harmonics and mass relations.
- Effective forces as curvature gradients.
- Computational and information-theoretic limits.
- Correspondence with known physical constants.
- RCA-style coherence architectures.
- Cryptographic or computational constructions based on curvature dynamics.

---

## Compression Summary

WCT requires only a wavefield, confinement, and nonlinear self-generated curvature feedback. Mass, coherent structure, spectral organization, and dimensional stability follow as consequences.

## One-Sentence Novelty Claim

Wave Confinement Theory proposes that inertial mass, curvature-mediated stability, and constraints on spatial dimensionality arise jointly from self-confined, phase-coherent standing-wave solutions governed by a nonlinear curvature-feedback operator acting on a wavefield, rather than being imposed as background structures or empirical inputs.
