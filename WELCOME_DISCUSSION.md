# Welcome to the WCT Research Discussions

This forum is for serious, inspectable discussion of Wave Confinement Theory and its supporting computational research.

The goal is not to reward agreement. The goal is to make claims easier to question, reproduce, formalize, falsify, correct, and improve.

## Where to post

- **geometry_of_resonance**: theory-wide questions, derivations, numerical models, physical interpretation, empirical proposals, and cross-repository claim dependencies.
- **NIST**: public atomic-line data, the Fe II logarithmic-wavenumber scan, null models, bin stability, sensitivity analysis, and independent reproductions.
- **wct-sympy**: symbolic equations, dimensional closure, residual checks, assumptions, domain safety, and executable audit verdicts.
- **wct-lean**: precise theorem statements, support lemmas, proof attempts, counterexamples, and the gap between informal claims and formally checked results.

## What belongs here

Questions, criticism, alternate derivations, negative results, failed reproductions, counterexamples, proposed experiments, independent implementations, sensitivity studies, and clearly labeled speculative ideas are all welcome.

Please identify the exact equation, claim, file, commit, dataset, command, or artifact being discussed. For computational results, include enough information for another person to rerun the work. For formal results, state every encoded assumption. For empirical claims, describe controls, uncertainty, trial correction, and failure criteria.

## Evidence labels

Use the narrowest accurate description:

- formally checked;
- symbolically checked;
- computationally reproduced;
- independently implemented;
- numerically supported;
- empirically tested;
- failed or falsified;
- incomplete;
- exploratory;
- speculative.

A passing software test establishes only the encoded test. A reproduced computation is not automatically an independent scientific confirmation. A formal support lemma proves only its precise statement under its encoded assumptions.

## Community standard

Be direct about technical weaknesses without attacking people. Preserve null and unfavorable results. Separate established mathematics from numerical evidence, empirical evidence, and interpretation. When a discussion produces a bounded implementation task, move that task to an issue or pull request and link the discussion.

The strongest contribution may be a successful reproduction, a clean failure, a decisive counterexample, or a better-controlled test.
