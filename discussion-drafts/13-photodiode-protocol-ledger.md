# A Timestamped Protocol for Long-Lived Harmonic-State Induction in Photodiodes

**Author:** Richard J. Reyes  
**Published:** December 2025  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17957713  
**Category:** Experiments

## Main result

I published this prediction and protocol ledger before the next photodiode experiments.

The predicted effect is a long-lived harmonic response that remains after the external drive is removed. The document fixes the apparatus, excitation sequence, expected signature, controls, timing, and failure conditions before the result is known.

This creates a timestamped experimental record and prevents the success criteria from being changed after data collection.

## What this adds to the corpus

The ledger converts the broader cavity and resonance observations into a preregistered detector-level test. It separates a prospective WCT prediction from retrospective interpretation.

## Direct tests

- Define quantitative amplitude, frequency, and persistence thresholds.
- Run dark, sham-drive, blocked-light, and detector-swap controls.
- Measure capacitance, thermal recovery, amplifier settling, and persistent photoconductivity.
- Release raw unfiltered traces with synchronized environmental channels.
- Blind trial labels until the analysis pipeline is frozen.
- Require replication on a second detector and acquisition chain.
