# A Stability-Based Upper Bound of Three Physical Spatial Dimensions

**Author:** Richard J. Reyes  
**Published:** August 13, 2025  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17081283  
**Category:** Core WCT / Mass and Geometry

## Main result

I derived a hard dimensionality result inside Wave Confinement Theory.

For the stated field functional, regularization, admissible perturbations, and Lyapunov conditions, nontrivial stable confined equilibria exist only for n ≤ 3. For n ≥ 4, the corresponding equilibria become unstable saddles.

The result gives a physical stability mechanism for why persistent confined structure can exist in three spatial dimensions but fails in higher dimensions.

## What this adds to the corpus

Rather than assuming the observed number of spatial dimensions, the paper connects dimensionality to the existence of stable localized states. The n ≤ 3 result also links confinement, mass formation, and spatial geometry within the same stability framework.

## Direct tests

- Recalculate the complete second variation and Hessian spectrum.
- Expand the perturbation space beyond the original ansatz.
- Test alternative boundary and decay conditions.
- Determine whether other admissible field functionals evade the bound.
- Reproduce the stability transition numerically for n = 1 through n = 5.
