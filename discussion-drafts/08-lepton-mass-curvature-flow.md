# A Curvature-Flow Origin of the Charged-Lepton Mass Hierarchy

**Author:** Richard J. Reyes  
**Published:** March 10, 2026  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18936949  
**Category:** Mass and Geometry

## Main result

I developed a model in which logarithmic curvature flow produces localized filament states with a discrete mass hierarchy.

The electron, muon, and tau emerge as related winding states of the same underlying field structure. The resulting geometry produces the observed hierarchy and a Koide-like relationship between the three charged-lepton masses.

The mass spectrum is therefore treated as organized confinement geometry rather than three unrelated input values.

## What this adds to the corpus

This paper supplies a dynamical mechanism for the mass-triplet geometry developed elsewhere in WCT. It links curvature flow, filament localization, winding number, and the charged-lepton spectrum in one construction.

## Direct tests

- Count every continuous and discrete choice used in the mass map.
- Test whether the state assignments are unique.
- Perturb the flow and determine whether the three filament states remain stable.
- Freeze the construction before applying it to another fermion family.
- Derive the mass relation directly from the field action.
