# A Log-Periodic Deformation of C₉(q²) in B⁰ → K*⁰ μ⁺μ⁻

**Author:** Richard J. Reyes  
**Published:** April 23, 2026  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19705254  
**Category:** Open-Data Tests / Particle Phenomenology

## Main result

I developed a q²-dependent deformation of the Wilson coefficient C₉ based on the curvature structure of Wave Confinement Theory.

Instead of representing the B⁰ → K*⁰ μ⁺μ⁻ anomaly only through a constant offset, the paper introduces a log-periodic correction that can produce localized structure across the measured spectrum.

The model turns the WCT interpretation into a direct phenomenological test. The predicted structure can be compared against constant-C₉ fits, nonlocal hadronic effects, charmonium leakage, resonance structure, detector response, and statistical fluctuation.

## What this adds to the corpus

This paper connects the curvature and discrete-scale structure developed in the core WCT papers to a specific flavor-physics observable. It also defines a pathway from the theoretical operator to a likelihood-level comparison with public or collaboration data.

## Direct tests

- Compare constant, smooth, resonance-based, and log-periodic C₉ models under the same likelihood.
- Test stability under q² bin changes and exclusion of resonance-adjacent regions.
- Include look-elsewhere and parameter penalties.
- Repeat the fixed analysis on independent B⁰ → K*⁰ μ⁺μ⁻ datasets.
