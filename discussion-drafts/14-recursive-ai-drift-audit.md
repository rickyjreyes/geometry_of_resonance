# Recursive AI Drift: A 2025 Prediction Timeline Compared with Later Evidence

**Author:** Richard J. Reyes  
**Published:** May 2026  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20142976  
**Category:** AI Architecture

## Main result

In 2025 I published a timeline of predicted failure modes involving recursive AI systems.

These included objective drift, evaluator drift, curriculum drift, problem drift, self-reinforcing synthetic data, and feedback between generated outputs and future training.

This audit compares those timestamped predictions with research and reported observations published afterward. The analysis separates direct matches, partial corroboration, conceptual overlap, and unresolved predictions.

## What this adds to the corpus

The audit provides a chronology and evidence ledger for the earlier Resonance-Confinement Architecture work. It tests whether the predicted drift mechanisms appeared later in independent technical literature and public reporting.

## Direct tests

- Preserve the original 2025 wording beside every later comparison.
- Grade specificity, timing, independence, and causal similarity separately.
- Include failed and unresolved predictions, not only matches.
- Distinguish replication of a mechanism from broad conceptual overlap.
- Repeat the audit with independent reviewers using fixed criteria.
