# A Model-Relative Pᵂᶜᶜ ≠ NPᵂᶜᶜ Separation

**Author:** Richard J. Reyes  
**Published:** May 7, 2025  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17743607  
**Category:** Computation and Cryptography

## Main result

I derived a P versus NP separation inside a curvature-bounded wave-computation model.

The model includes finite precision, bounded curvature, finite propagation, restricted state preparation, and physically admissible measurement. Under those constraints, polynomial-time construction and polynomial-time verification separate into distinct model-relative classes.

The result applies to Pᵂᶜᶜ and NPᵂᶜᶜ. It does not rely on unbounded analog precision or unrestricted state preparation.

## What this adds to the corpus

This paper defines the restricted complexity result that follows the classical-equivalence baseline. Together, the two computation papers separate ordinary discrete simulation from a physically constrained wave model with its own resource structure.

## Direct tests

- Formalize the machine, input encoding, uniformity, and acceptance conditions.
- Verify closure and reduction properties of Pᵂᶜᶜ and NPᵂᶜᶜ.
- Audit whether hardness is introduced through representation or state preparation.
- Test alternative discretizations and curvature bounds.
- Compare the lower-bound argument with known restricted-model separations.
