# Rest Energy from Density-Weighted Loop Curvature

**Author:** Richard J. Reyes  
**Published:** November 11, 2025  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20533537  
**Category:** Mass and Geometry

## Main result

I developed a covariant locking principle in which rest energy emerges from density-weighted curvature integrated around a closed field loop.

The massive state is modeled as a confined field configuration with internal phase flow that closes on itself. The loop geometry produces a persistent invariant connected to the rest-energy scale of the state.

The proposal links density, curvature, topology, and confinement in one mass-generating quantity.

## What this adds to the corpus

This paper gives the WCT mass program a compact covariant object. It connects the solenoidal effective-mass picture with the loop and winding geometry used in the lepton and Koide papers.

## Direct tests

- Verify Lorentz covariance of the full construction.
- Prove invariance under loop reparametrization.
- Derive conservation or controlled evolution from the field equations.
- Check dimensional normalization and limiting behavior.
- Test open, linked, knotted, and perturbed loops.
- Compare the resulting rest energy with the total stress-energy integral.
