# Log-Spectral Structure in Open B⁰ → K*⁰ μ⁺μ⁻ Candidate Spectra

**Author:** Richard J. Reyes  
**Published:** May 9, 2026  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20164333  
**Category:** Open-Data Tests / Particle Phenomenology

## Main result

I analyzed open B⁰ → K*⁰ μ⁺μ⁻ candidate spectra in logarithmic coordinates and found recurring spectral structure with a Koide-like winding pattern.

The structure is not limited to one visual binning choice and produces a specific geometric interpretation connected to Wave Confinement Theory.

Its physical significance can be tested directly against resonance contamination, background models, trial factors, coordinate transforms, and independent data.

## What this adds to the corpus

This paper provides the open-data counterpart to the C₉ deformation model. One paper identifies the spectral structure; the other supplies a Wilson-coefficient mechanism capable of generating it.

## Direct tests

- Freeze the transform and peak-selection pipeline before new data are examined.
- Apply global rather than local significance.
- Remove or model resonance-adjacent regions.
- Compare against smooth, resonance-based, and randomized null spectra.
- Repeat the same procedure on an independent candidate sample.
