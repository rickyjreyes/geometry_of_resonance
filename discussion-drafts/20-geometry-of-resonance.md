**Category:** Core WCT

## Main result

This is the main foundation paper for Wave Confinement Theory.

The framework begins with a continuous field and the curvature operator:

Wψ = −∇²ψ / ψ

Nonlinear finite-band selection, curvature, topology, and resonance produce stable confined states.

Mass emerges from the energy and geometry of confinement. Forces emerge from interactions between confined states. Spacetime structure is connected to the field’s curvature and phase organization.

## What this adds to the corpus

This paper establishes the central language and physical program used by the later PFF, mass, dimensionality, constants, Koide, experiment, and computation papers. It is the broad conceptual entry point to the corpus.
