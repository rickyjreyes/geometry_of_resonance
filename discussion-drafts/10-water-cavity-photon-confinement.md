# Long-Lived Photon Resonance Confinement in Water Cavities

**Author:** Richard J. Reyes  
**Published:** May 17, 2025  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17206381  
**Category:** Experiments

## Main result

I observed a persistent photodetector response after optical and acoustic excitation of a water cavity.

The signal continued after the primary drive was removed and decayed over a much longer interval than the excitation timescale. The measured persistence is consistent with a long-lived confined resonance state.

The experiment establishes a specific signal, apparatus, and decay behavior that can now be separated from conventional material, detector, thermal, and electrical effects through targeted controls.

## What this adds to the corpus

This is the experimental branch of the WCT program. It tests whether driven cavities can enter persistent harmonic states rather than returning immediately to baseline after excitation.

## Direct tests

- Repeat with an empty cavity and with multiple fluids.
- Swap the photodetector, amplifier, cables, and data-acquisition chain.
- Block the optical path immediately after excitation.
- Record temperature, vibration, acoustic pickup, dark current, and raw voltage simultaneously.
- Use blinded trials and independent replication.
- Fit competing decay models before assigning a physical mechanism.
