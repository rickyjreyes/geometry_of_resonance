# Phase-Flux Field as the Axiomatic Substrate of Wave Confinement Theory

**Author:** Richard J. Reyes  
**Published:** September 8, 2025  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17578766  
**Category:** Core WCT

## Main result

This paper develops the mathematical substrate of Wave Confinement Theory.

The Phase-Flux Field includes zero-wave invariance, finite-k Lyapunov selection, nonlinear mode competition, shell quantization, and a D₄-to-continuum construction.

The central result is that stable localized structure can emerge from a continuous field through finite-band selection and confinement. Particles are not inserted at the beginning. They arise as persistent organized states of the field.

## Core structure

The finite-band mechanism selects a preferred nonzero wave number k⋆ rather than allowing either the zero mode or arbitrarily high-frequency modes to dominate. Nonlinear competition then concentrates the evolving field into a restricted shell structure.

## What this adds to the corpus

PFF provides the axioms and stability machinery underneath the mass, dimensionality, Fourier-shell, and confinement papers. It is the natural technical starting point for evaluating WCT as a field theory.

## Direct tests

- Prove local and global well-posedness for the stated evolution.
- Verify the Lyapunov functional and finite-k stability interval.
- Derive shell spacing without relying on numerical selection.
- Test the D₄-to-continuum limit under refinement.
- Reproduce mode selection across grids, boundaries, and initial conditions.
