# Resonance-Confinement Architecture for Physically Bounded Advanced AI

**Author:** Richard J. Reyes  
**Published:** June 11, 2025  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17732661  
**Category:** AI Architecture

## Main result

I developed the Resonance-Confinement Architecture as a physically enforced approach to advanced AI containment.

The architecture limits recursion, energy, bandwidth, memory, state transitions, actuator access, and self-modification at the substrate level.

The core argument is that software rules alone may fail if a system can alter its own objectives, evaluators, representations, or training environment. Physical bounds reduce the reachable failure space even when internal software behavior becomes difficult to interpret.

## What this adds to the corpus

RCA applies the broader WCT principle of stable, bounded state evolution to machine intelligence. It treats safety as an architectural and physical constraint problem rather than only an instruction-following problem.

## Direct tests

- Build a formal threat model for bypasses and side channels.
- Specify which safety properties are enforced physically and which remain software-dependent.
- Verify recursion, memory, energy, and actuator bounds at the hardware interface.
- Test shutdown, recovery, and degraded-mode behavior under adversarial conditions.
- Compare the architecture against sandboxing, capability control, and formal verification.
