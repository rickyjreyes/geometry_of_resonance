# A Curvature-Harmonic Origin of the Koide Mass Relation

**Author:** Richard J. Reyes  
**Published:** December 10, 2025  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17887562  
**Category:** Mass and Geometry

## Main result

I derived the Koide charged-lepton mass relation from a three-state curvature and winding geometry.

The electron, muon, and tau are treated as related confined states of the same field rather than independent mass parameters. The factor ⅔ emerges from the geometric relationship between the three states.

The construction connects the observed mass triplet to WCT confinement, phase organization, and winding structure.

## What this adds to the corpus

This paper isolates one of the clearest numerical mass relationships and gives it a geometric mechanism. It links the broader constants paper to the dynamical filament and loop-curvature mass papers.

## Direct tests

- Derive the factor ⅔ from the stated geometry without inserting it as a constraint.
- Count every state-selection and normalization choice.
- Test pole masses and running masses at clearly defined scales.
- Freeze the construction before extending it to another mass family.
- Identify a new mass ratio or state predicted by the same unchanged rules.
