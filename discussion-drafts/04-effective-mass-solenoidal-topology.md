# Effective Mass from Solenoidal Topology of Vibrational Energy

**Author:** Richard J. Reyes  
**Published:** October 27, 2025  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17459463  
**Category:** Mass and Geometry

## Main result

I developed a field model in which effective mass emerges from localized vibrational energy arranged in a stable solenoidal structure.

The confined state carries finite energy, internal circulation, curvature, and resistance to acceleration. The paper connects those properties to an effective inertial response rather than treating mass only as an independent input parameter.

The key proposal is that topology and confinement geometry determine how stored field energy responds to translation and perturbation.

## What this adds to the corpus

This paper gives the mass program a concrete geometric state: a closed, circulating, localized configuration whose inertial behavior can be calculated. It connects the general WCT curvature operator to a specific solenoidal topology.

## Direct tests

- Derive the collective-coordinate inertial term from the field action.
- Test stability against radial, angular, and topology-changing perturbations.
- Verify covariance and energy-momentum conservation.
- Separate the topological contribution from ordinary total field energy.
- Simulate acceleration and collision of the localized state.
